PRODUCT SLOT

cl1nical | AI Clinic Management System

cl1nical is an AI clinic management system that I built. 3 clinics use it today on a free trial in local server. Doctors, receptionists, admins, and patients work from one place for appointments, records, and prescriptions. Clinics spend less on paper and pens, and patients save time. One clinic owner messaged me that his patients and his receptionist are very happy; the workflow is easy and fast, and he plans to buy a subscription soon.
